Grenada’s 51stIndependence Day AfterParty

Imperial Citizenship sponsored the after party for Grenada’s 51st Independence Day, following the official celebrations hosted by IMA Grenada and the Consulate General of Grenada in Dubai.

Imperial Citizenship sponsored the after party for Grenada’s 51st Independence Day in Dubai on 19 February 2025, held at Agora Cocktail Bar and Social Club at The Dubai EDITION, Downtown Dubai.

The evening followed the official celebrations at the Marriott Resort Palm Jumeirah, hosted by IMA Grenada and the Consulate General of Grenada in Dubai.

Among the guests
  • H.E. Ziya Rahaman, Consul General of Grenada to the UAE
  • Hon. Dennis Cornwall, Minister of Finance, Grenada
  • H.E. Justin Kareem Hawley, Ambassador of St Kitts and Nevis to the UAE
  • Mr Thomas Anthony, CEO, IMA Grenada
  • H.E. Murillo Fallas
  • H.E. James Harris
  • H.E. Andrew Tsepo Lebona
  • Mr Zaid Al Hindi, Founder and Group CEO, Imperial Holdings Group

Source: Press release, 19 February 2025 (Marketing Team SharePoint)
