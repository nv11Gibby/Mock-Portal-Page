Six things worththinking about beforea first conversation

About 1 minute read

Draft
Draft, awaiting approval. Final copy to be approved before publication. Author and publication date: To confirm: to confirm

You do not need any documents to begin. A little thought about six questions helps us give you useful options straight away.

ShareinWA@

Nobody arrives wanting a visa for its own sake. They arrive wanting a different next chapter. A little thought about the questions below helps a first conversation move quickly to options that fit.

Your family

Who would be included, and their ages.

Where you are now

Where you live, work and hold citizenship today.

What you want

Which of the six ambitions matter most, and why: live, build, structure, invest, protect or experience.

A budget range

A rough range is enough. We will show what fits.

Your timing

Any dates that matter: school terms, a move, a company launch.

Your history

Any past applications or refusals. Being open helps us help you.

Not sure about some of them? That is what the first conversation is for.
