# Hero messaging options for Founder approval (v6, deliverable 2)

Three copywriters wrote from three angles (the Founder's direction refined; the institution; the two cities), producing six variants. Two independent judges, one reading as a private banker and one as a brand editor, scored every variant out of 60 on clarity, restraint, warmth, aspiration, institutional fit and truth. Both chose the Founder's direction. Option A is in the prototype; B and C are ready to swap in if preferred. "Two Cities. One Horizon." stays as the secondary signature in every option.

## Option A (recommended): A world of opportunity. Built around relationships.

**Headline:** A world of opportunity. / Built around relationships.

**Eyebrow:** Imperial Holdings Group · Dubai · Lagos

**Supporting copy:** An international group bringing together mobility, private client services, real estate and professional partnerships. One connected ecosystem, serving ambitions that extend beyond borders. One named person looks after each relationship for the long term, with the judgement to say plainly what is possible, and what is not.

**Signature:** the small line above the closing headline (the Start a Relationship band after the Dubai and Lagos map), and in small type in the footer beneath the Group name; not in the hero

**Calls to action:** Arrange a first conversation · Discuss becoming a Partner · Open a strategic conversation

**The Group:** One institution. / Five specialist divisions.. Imperial Holdings Group is the parent of five specialist divisions, led from Dubai and Lagos. Imperial Citizenship, the flagship, advises families and founders on second residency and citizenship by investment. Four further divisions, two of them in preparation, extend that relationship. Families, and the professionals who advise them, are served by one group, through one named person.

**Divisions:**

- Where the Group began. The flagship, and often the first step: second residency and citizenship by investment, carried from first conversation to completed application.

- For the professionals families trust. Lawyers, bankers, advisers and family offices who introduce Clients to the Group, on written terms, with clear updates. Your Client stays yours.

- The relationship, continued. Ongoing support for existing Imperial Clients after the first service is complete: relocation, schools, structuring and introductions, through one point of contact.

- Property in Dubai, in preparation. Property advisory, not yet launched: search, selection and purchase support, coordinated with Imperial Citizenship where a home and residency meet. Interest can be registered now.

- Learning and standards, in preparation. The Group's learning arm, in preparation: training and certification to one shared standard, for our own people and for Partner teams.

**Closing:** The first conversation / is for listening.. Tell us where you are, and where you would like to be. A senior adviser will read what you write, set out what is possible in writing, and reply personally. Nothing is decided until you are ready.

**Why:** The Founder's headline and supporting copy, with one word changed ('serving' in place of 'built to support', so 'built' is not repeated beneath 'Built around relationships') and a third sentence that makes 'relationships' concrete through the one named person the site already promises. Both judges scored it highest of six: open rather than self-regarding, truthful, and the page a banker could forward to a client without a caveat.

The Founder's original supporting copy, if preferred word for word: "An international group bringing together mobility, private client services, real estate and professional partnerships. One connected ecosystem, built to support ambitions that extend beyond borders."

Imperial Holdings Group is the parent institution of five specialist divisions: citizenship and residency, a professional Partner network, a Private Office for existing Clients, and, in preparation, property advisory and an academy for learning and standards. Led from Dubai and Lagos, one team works to one standard, so a family explains itself once and is known for years.

**Divisions:**

- Where the Group began Second residency and citizenship by investment, carried from first conversation to completed application. The flagship, and the Group's main business today.

- The professional Partner network Lawyers, bankers, advisers and family offices who introduce Clients to the Group, on written terms, with clear updates. Your Client stays yours.

- Looking after existing Clients One point of contact for existing Clients after their first service: relocation, schools, structuring and introductions, through trusted Partners and qualified specialists.

- Property in Dubai, in preparation Property advisory, not yet launched: search, selection and purchase support, coordinated with Imperial Citizenship where a home and residency meet.

Each division line says truthfully what operates and what is in preparation; the signature signs off the closing band.

**Divisions:**

Because the headline now says 'two cities', the signature moves to the closing band, where it opens the Start a Relationship invitation in full.

## The judges' synthesis

**Judge 1 (private banker):** The Founder's direction wins on merit: Option 3 has the stronger body, but its headline declares the institution rather than showing it. Go forward with Option 1's headline, eyebrow and signature placement. Support: the Founder's two sentences, 'serving ambitions', then 'One named person looks after each relationship for the long term, with the judgement to say plainly what is possible, and what is not', from Option 3. Group intro: Option 1's headline over Option 3's copy, 'parent' replacing 'parent institution'. Divisions: Option 1's headlines, 'in preparation' for both unlaunched divisions, Option 3's Partners Club and Private Office lines, Option 1's others. Calls to action from Option 3. Closing: Option 1, adding 'and set out what is possible in writing'.

**Judge 2 (brand editor):** Go forward with Option 1; the Founder's prior wins on merit, not deference. Headline: 'A world of opportunity.' / 'Built around relationships.' Supporting copy: Option 1's three sentences, with 'serving' in place of 'built to support'. Eyebrow and signature placement as Option 1: closing-band eyebrow after the map, repeated in the footer. Division headlines from Option 1, with full stops; lines from Option 1 for Citizenship, Private Office and Academy, Option 3's Partners Club line ('on written terms, with clear updates. Your Client stays yours.'), and Option 3's Real Estate line under 'Property in Dubai, in preparation.' Calls to action: 'Arrange a first conversation', 'Discuss becoming a Partner', 'Open a strategic conversation'. Closing: Option 1's headline, with Option 3's 'set out what is possible in writing' grafted into the body. Option 3 has the finest sentence; its headline is cold.

## Other variants considered

- **A world of opportunity. / One relationship at a time.** (Variant: one relationship at a time): Same page. Only line two changes, and the Founder's two sentences stand word for word with the third sentence added. 'One relationship at a time' replaces a construction metaphor with a human one, and says how the Group works rather than what it is organised around. It reads warmer, at a slight cost in institutional weight, so it is offered second.
- **A steady hand / for a life across borders.** (The institution, variant headline): The variant keeps everything but the headline. A steady hand carries judgement and permanence in three words and reads more warmly than institution, while a life across borders names the Client's world without a sales claim. Choose it if the Founder finds institution too formal for the first line a visitor reads; the support, divisions and closing stay unchanged.
- **Led from Dubai and Lagos. / With the world in view.** (The View from Two Cities): Place is the Group's verifiable credential: a Dubai headquarters, a Lagos hub, and Clients whose lives already cross borders. Leading with the cities states a fact, not a boast; 'with the world in view' carries the horizon without brochure imagery. The signature sits where the eye lands next, the Founder's ecosystem copy follows intact, and each division is described as it stands.
