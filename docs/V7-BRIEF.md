# IHG V7: Founder and stakeholder experience review

Posted by the Founder's office in the IHG Website thread on 16 October 2026 (18:17 UTC). Saved verbatim; dashes replaced, HTML entities decoded.

Creative Direction, Institutional Trust and Operational Reality

Claude,
We have reviewed Imperial Holdings Group v6 from five perspectives:

1. The Founder building an international group.
2. A UHNW entrepreneur or family considering a relationship.
3. A respected professional adviser deciding whether to introduce a client.
4. A senior Imperial adviser representing the Group in a high-value conversation.
5. An Imperial employee responsible for delivering the promises made publicly.

Our conclusion: v6 represents meaningful functional and editorial progress, but the overall experience is not yet approved as the final institutional expression of Imperial Holdings Group.
We are not asking you to discard the useful v6 engineering.
We are asking for a deeper creative, commercial and operational review.

## A. First reconcile the actual build
The Render preview retrieved externally appears to show older homepage wording, while the supplied v6 source contains the new brand direction.
Verify the live deployment, commit hash, preview build and production build.
Give us one unambiguous, reviewable version.
Do not declare v7 ready until the Founder is reviewing the exact intended build.

## B. Conduct five independent stakeholder walkthroughs
For each persona, walk through the complete journey from first arrival to taking action.
Assess:
* First impression and perceived institutional credibility.
* Understanding of Imperial's actual value.
* Emotional confidence and trust.
* Relevance to that person's specific needs.
* Navigation and information discovery.
* Quality of evidence supporting the claims.
* Likelihood of initiating a relationship.
* Quality of the handoff after enquiry.
* Anything confusing, intimidating, unconvincing or unnecessary.

Do not produce generic UX commentary.
Identify the exact pages, sections, wording and interactions responsible for each finding.

## C. Reconsider the Group's creative direction
Explore three genuinely different creative directions.
IHG must have a distinct institutional identity while retaining shared Imperial brand DNA with IPC and IPO.
Avoid assuming dark graphite, gold and oversized serif headlines are automatically the correct expression.
Explore greater architectural sophistication, stronger editorial storytelling, authentic people, meaningful photography and confident typography.
Present real desktop and mobile compositions for each direction.
Do not rebuild the entire site until the Founder chooses.

## D. Redesign the homepage narrative
The homepage should establish:
Who Imperial is, then why it exists, then why it can be trusted, then how the Group works, then who it serves, then why someone would begin a relationship.
Do not make the visitor understand all five divisions before they understand the value of Imperial.
Show how one relationship can connect different needs over time.
Use a clearly fictional illustration where appropriate.
Make the human relationship central and the organisational architecture supportive.

## E. Strengthen institutional evidence
Audit every credible proof point available from approved records.
Consider:
* Verified Imperial operating history.
* Founder and leadership experience.
* Real Dubai and Lagos presence.
* Real events and institutional engagements.
* Relevant specialist capabilities.
* Approved client experiences or anonymised examples.
* Genuine professional partnerships, where disclosure is authorised.

Distinguish between verified evidence, information requiring confirmation and material unsuitable for public disclosure.
Never invent client names, relationships, institutional endorsements or operating statistics.
We want confidence through evidence, not exaggerated claims.

## F. Design three differentiated audience journeys
Private Clients: A discreet and reassuring introduction, with minimal initial information required.
Professional Partners: Clear relationship protection, professionalism, accountability and a route to IPC.
Institutions and Strategic Relationships: A credible, direct route for governments, developers and serious institutional counterparties.
Do not make all three audiences experience the same generic contact form with different labels.
Each should feel recognised and appropriately served.

## G. Test the promises against Imperial OS
Map each major website promise to an operational owner and system.
For example:
* Named relationship owner.
* Lead verification and qualification.
* Adviser assignment.
* Existing-client recognition through IRI.
* Partner-of-record protection.
* Specialist introductions.
* Processing handover.
* Client success and ongoing relationship management.

For each promise, identify whether it is currently operational, partially implemented, planned or unverified.
Do not represent an architectural aspiration as an active service capability.

## H. Remove unfinished institutional signals
Audit all visible "To confirm" chips, placeholder links, draft content, unavailable booking links and future division features.
Classify each as:
* Must resolve before launch.
* Should be hidden until ready.
* Can remain publicly visible with elegant, accurate wording.

Our preferred approach is to publish fewer, excellent pages rather than expose unfinished internal processes.

## I. Preserve the successful v6 work
Retain and protect:
* Shared ecosystem architecture.
* Functional navigation and search.
* Appropriate lead-routing foundations.
* Privacy-by-design principles.
* Accurate division statuses.
* Copy and claims governance.
* Accessibility and responsive improvements.
* Honest prototype and production distinctions.

Do not regress existing tested functionality.

## J. Deliver the Founder Decision Package
Before implementing v7, provide:
1. Independent assessment of v6.
2. Five stakeholder journey reviews.
3. Ten highest-impact experience problems, ranked.
4. Three distinct creative directions with desktop/mobile examples.
5. Recommended homepage narrative.
6. Trust and evidence improvement plan.
7. Revised audience-specific relationship journeys.
8. Website promise-to-operational-capability matrix.
9. Exact elements to preserve, redesign or remove.
10. Launch-critical dependencies and owners.
11. Your recommended direction and the decisions needed from the Founder.

Stop at the Founder decision stage. Do not automatically implement your recommendation.
Our standard is not whether the website looks luxurious.
Our standard is whether an accomplished person, family or professional would consider Imperial a credible institution worthy of a long-term relationship.
The website must also be something every Imperial adviser is proud to share and every Imperial employee is equipped to stand behind.
