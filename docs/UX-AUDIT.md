# Interaction and experience audit (v6 brief, section 8)

Imperial Holdings Group website, branch `ihg-site`, audited from the worktree `/home/claude/mock-portal-page/.claude/worktrees/v6-ux` (prerendered `dist/`, served locally on port 8902). Nothing in the repository was changed in this phase. Date of audit: 15 October 2026.

Evidence files live in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-claude-mock-portal-page/1e125475-d510-5add-ae24-7eb5bf27f2e4/scratchpad/v6/ux/` (called OUT below): `audit.json` (all measurements), `links.json` and `dom-links.json` (link check), `lighthouse/*.json` (Lighthouse 13.5, mobile defaults), `a11y-src.log` (axe run from the earlier phase), the scripts `audit.js`, `audit2.js`, `contact-ok.js`, `hash-check.js`, `nojs-check.js`, `links.js`, `lh6.js`, and 87 screenshots under `shots/`. Screenshot paths below are relative to OUT.

The rule applied throughout is the brief's own: every interaction should either improve understanding, increase confidence or help the visitor take an appropriate next step. Anything that does none of these is marked for removal or simplification. The features the brief protects (site search, responsive navigation, division exploration and the Journal) are kept.

## 1. Summary

- 56 interactions were reviewed across 13 routes on a 1440 x 900 desktop and a 390 x 844 phone (plus 375 and 360 px header checks). The site is well built: no console errors, no layout shift (CLS 0 on all 26 route and viewport combinations), 0 axe violations on every page in both viewports, every visible control reachable by keyboard, focus visible everywhere, a working skip link, a complete reduced-motion mode, and forms that validate, announce, fall back and keep drafts correctly.
- The problem is quantity of motion rather than quality. A first visitor to the home page sees a black veil for 1.6 seconds, then a 1.4 second headline reveal, then a hero that stays pinned for 675 px of scrolling, then 63 staggered reveals over 16 screens of page (22 on a phone), with a film grain layer, a pulsing clock dot, a scroll cue, a progress bar, magnetic buttons and a world map with pinging pins. Under Lighthouse's mobile simulation the home page's largest contentful paint is 4.9 s and the element it measures is the hero lead paragraph, because the headline itself is delayed by the veil and the reveal. If `app.js` fails or JavaScript is off, the home page never leaves the veil (`shots/nojs-home.png`).
- 34 recommended changes follow in section 9: 8 removals, 17 simplifications and 9 items to keep with a fix. Five are high severity: remove the intro veil; replace the pinned parallax hero with a plain full-height hero; cut the reveal system to one short fade; raise the contrast of small text over photographs (eyebrows and breadcrumbs fail on several heroes); and fix the header on phones, where the WhatsApp button is squeezed to 13 to 23 px wide.
- Links: 0 broken internal links in the built site, 0 missing assets, 0 broken anchors. 25 unique external URLs could not be checked from this sandbox (the proxy refuses outbound CONNECT); two were confirmed reachable by a separate fetch (imperialcitizenship.com and one Unsplash credit page) and the rest, all wa.me and LinkedIn share URLs, follow the documented formats and remain to confirm from a normal connection. 402 placeholder links (`href="#"` with `data-tbc`, 8 on a typical page) stand in for the four division websites not yet built; each shows a "Link to confirm" toast rather than failing silently.
- Counts: interactions reviewed 56; removals 8; simplifications 17; broken links 0; unreachable external links 25 from the sandbox (2 of them confirmed reachable by other means, 23 unverified).

## 4. Navigation

- Header: About, Our ecosystem (menu), Our Network, Journal, Contact, search, Enquire now. "Our Network" is not a page: it routes to the home page and scrolls 3,907 px to `#network` (`nav.our network link`). From any other page that is a two-step jump into the middle of a long page. Simplify (section 9, item 8).
- Header hiding: after 520 px of downward scroll the header, and on phones the menu button, are off screen until the visitor scrolls up (`shots/desktop-home-header-hidden.png`). Simplify (item 7).
- Breadcrumbs are correct on division and event pages; the skip link focuses `main`; a click on the current page's own nav item scrolls to the top rather than re-rendering; the browser back button restores scroll (1,470 px) and focus; old `#/about` style links are rewritten to clean URLs by the inline head script (verified with `hash-check.js`).
- 404 and error pages: the 404 page has a heading, three links and the office contacts; `robots` is `noindex, nofollow`. The local server answered unknown paths with the fallback page (status 200) in the second run and with 404 in the first (the two runs used different server flags); the real status depends on the host honouring `dist/_redirects`. To confirm on the host.
- House finder: "2 of 5 houses" for a founder who wants to build, with a CTA to `/contact?about=citizenship&goals=build`. Keep (`shots/desktop-divisions-finder.png`).
- Comparison table: 980 px minimum width inside a 341 px phone viewport with a "Swipe sideways to compare" hint; the sticky row headers help, but the visitor sees one column at a time under the action bar (`shots/mobile-divisions-compare.png`). Simplify (item 9).
- Division CTAs: "Visit the Partners Club website" is a placeholder (`#`, data-tbc) in both the glance panel and the band, with "Speak to us" beside it; four of six sites in the footer and mega menu are the same. These are honest placeholders with a toast; they must be resolved or hidden before launch (item 34).
- Journal and events: filters update the URL (`/insights?cat=Mobility`), the event page has a pending video slot ("To confirm: Highlights clip to be supplied"), a consent-gated gallery (0 images until guests confirm), 4 share buttons, 3 related items and previous/next links. Keep (protected).

## 9. Recommended changes

Severity: high (changes what a first visitor experiences or fails a standard), medium (measurable cost or friction), low (polish). Decision: keep, simplify or remove. "Where" names the file and function or selector in the worktree.

1. High, remove: the intro veil. Why: 1.6 s of black on the first visit, the hero headline and reveals held back 1.6 s, LCP 4.9 s on mobile, and a permanent black screen if `app.js` does not run (`body.veiled` is baked into the prerendered home). It adds nothing a visitor can use. Evidence: `extras.first visit desktop/mobile` (veil until 1,613 / 1,603 ms), `shots/desktop-veil-900ms.png`, `shots/nojs-home.png`, `lighthouse/home.json`. Where: `index.html` `#veil` markup; `assets/js/app.js:1853-1861` (intro veil block); `assets/css/site.css:781-792` (`.intro-veil`, `.veiled`, `.unveil`, the 1.6 s delay rule); the build's prerender of `body.veiled`. How: delete the markup, the block and the rules; drop `ihg-intro` from sessionStorage use.
2. High, simplify: the pinned parallax hero. Why: 675 px (desktop) and 422 px (phone) of scrolling during which nothing new appears, plus a scroll-linked transform on the largest image; the brief asks for calm. Evidence: `extras.home scroll budget` (section 1,575 / 1,266 px, pinned 675 / 422), `anim.pinned hero`, `shots/desktop-home-top.png`. Where: `site.css:185-190` (`.hh`, `.hh-pin`), `app.js:943` `onScroll` parallax branch, `app.js:255` `V.home` hero markup. How: make the reduced-motion layout the default (`.hh{height:auto}`, `.hh-pin{position:relative}`), remove the dusk and night layers from the scroll handler; keep one still photograph at 100svh.
3. High, simplify: masked headline and section reveals. Why: 1.4 s transforms with staggered delays on 63 blocks make every page feel slow to arrive, and after a route change most of the new page is invisible until scrolled. Evidence: `anim.reveal system` (18 masks, 63 data-r, 0.9 s / 1.4 s), `nav.desktop route transition home to about` (38 of 42 hidden on landing), `site.css:76-80`. Where: `site.css:76-80` and the `--t4`, `--t5` tokens in `shared/tokens.css`; `app.js:178` `mask()`, `app.js:908` `enhance()` (IntersectionObserver). How: replace with a single 0.3 s opacity fade with no translate and no per-block delay, or none; render everything visible on landing and keep the observer only for lazy work.
4. High, keep with a fix: contrast of small text over photographs. Why: eyebrows and breadcrumbs fall below 4.5:1 on several heroes and the Journal headline falls below 3:1 on the phone. Evidence: `a11y.contrast *` (home eyebrow p90 3.42; Citizenship breadcrumb mobile mean 3.57; Partners Club breadcrumb mobile mean 2.50; Journal mobile eyebrow mean 4.29, headline p90 2.95), `shots/mobile-contrast-divisions-partners-club.png`, `shots/mobile-contrast-journal.png`. Where: `site.css:209` (`.ph::after` gradient), `site.css:190` (`.hh-pin::after`), `site.css:39` (`.eyebrow`), `site.css:215-218` (`.crumb`). How: deepen the top of the hero gradient to at least rgba(14,13,11,.85) behind the breadcrumb and eyebrow, or place them on a solid band; use `--text` rather than `--soft` for breadcrumb links over photographs; re-measure with `photoContrast`.
5. High, keep with a fix: the header on phones. Why: the WhatsApp button is squeezed to 23 px at 390 px and 13 px at 375 and 360 px, below the 24 px minimum, because the 221 px wordmark and 32 px gaps take the row. Evidence: `extras.header at 390/375/360`, `a11y.touch /` (`button#waBtn 23x44`), `shots/mobile-home-top.png`. Where: `site.css:97` (`.hdr-in` gap), `site.css:99` (`.brand img`), `site.css:1175-1187` (`.wa-btn`). How: `flex: none; min-width: 44px` on `.wa-btn`, a smaller gap on narrow screens, and either the crown mark instead of the full wordmark under 420 px or the WhatsApp entry moved into the action bar only.
6. Medium, simplify: the mega menu. Why: opening on hover with a full-height scrim means crossing the navigation darkens the page; the 1.6 s preview zoom and three-stage stagger slow it. Evidence: `overlays.mega` (open 424 ms on hover, scrim 900 px, hidden after 1,179 ms), `shots/desktop-mega-open.png`. Where: `app.js:1101-1102` `openMega` / `closeMega` and the hover listeners that call them, `site.css:115-141`. How: open on click, Enter or ArrowDown only; remove `.mega::after`; one 0.2 s fade; keep the division list, descriptions and the site links (responsive navigation is protected).
7. Medium, simplify: header hiding on scroll. Why: on a phone the menu button disappears with the header after 520 px and the visitor must scroll up to find it; the brief asks for simple navigation. ## 10. Assumptions and limits

- The audit was run against the prerendered `dist/` on a local static server; POSTs were answered by the test server and the verify function was mocked. No live service was contacted.
- External links could not be fetched from this sandbox; two were confirmed by a separate fetch tool and the rest follow documented URL formats. They remain to confirm from a normal connection.
- Lighthouse figures use its default mobile simulation (slow 4G, 4x CPU slowdown) against an uncompressed local server; absolute numbers will differ on the host, the relative findings (render-blocking CSS, LCP delayed by the veil and reveals, oversized card images) will not.
- The figures band's numbers (10+, 600+, 2,000+, 20+, 100+) are held by the claims audit; only the count-up animation is judged here.
- Contrast over photographs was measured on the approved fallback hero photographs; if the final hero photograph changes, the hero measurements must be repeated.
- The contact flow failure reported by the first automated run was the script's omission (Partners Club route requires firm and profession); the flow works.
