# Brand and digital experience refinement brief (v6)

The Founder's mandate as received on 15 October 2026 in the IHG Website thread (the two dashes in the original are written as colons or commas here, as the house copy rules require). It follows the v5 refinement brief (docs/REFINEMENT-BRIEF.md); the rules of that brief stay in force: no invented facts, placeholders marked To confirm, no claims of guaranteed approval or government influence, British English, the site stays noindex until launch, nothing sent to a live service without approval.

IMPERIAL HOLDINGS GROUP: WORLD-CLASS BRAND AND DIGITAL EXPERIENCE REFINEMENT
Review the current IHG website at:
https://ihg-website-preview.onrender.com/
The Founder has reviewed the website and wants to elevate the messaging, visual restraint, institutional credibility and quality of the visitor experience.
This is a refinement mandate, not a request to rebuild the website.
1. Reposition the Group
Imperial Holdings Group is the parent institution of five specialist divisions:

* Imperial Citizenship
* Imperial Partners Club
* Imperial Private Office
* Imperial Real Estate
* Imperial Academy

The Group must feel established, international, sophisticated, relationship-led and trustworthy.
The website should be comparable in editorial quality and restraint to exceptional private banking, family office and international advisory brands.
It must not resemble a generic corporate holding company, citizenship sales website, luxury lifestyle magazine or event-management business.
2. Rewrite the entire public-facing website
Review every headline, description, CTA, menu label, footer statement, form question, search result and page introduction.
Develop a consistent institutional tone of voice.
The messaging should be:

* Confident without arrogance.
* Sophisticated without pretension.
* Aspirational without exaggeration.
* Clear without sounding transactional.
* Warm and human without becoming casual.

Avoid excessive references to luxury, exclusivity, elite status, wealth and VIP terminology.
Use simple, intelligent sentences that communicate genuine value.
Preferred hero direction:
A world of opportunity. Built around relationships.
Supporting copy:
"An international group bringing together mobility, private client services, real estate and professional partnerships. One connected ecosystem, built to support ambitions that extend beyond borders."
Preserve "Two Cities. One Horizon." as a secondary brand signature.
Present alternative copy options for Founder approval before finalizing.
3. Change the homepage photography
Remove the large people-focused event photograph from the homepage hero.
Replace it with an elegant architectural, cinematic or abstract visual that communicates international perspective and permanence.
Do not use excessive gold, generic handshake imagery, artificial wealth symbolism or cliched luxury visuals.
Use real Imperial event photographs selectively further down the page.
Target three to four carefully selected event images on the homepage.
Create a separate curated events and Journal experience for deeper stories.
Keep leadership photographs on the About or Leadership page, rather than dominating the homepage.
4. Elevate the five divisions
Give each division a distinctive but consistent presentation.
Use sophisticated headlines and concise descriptions that explain its role within the ecosystem.
Keep all five visually connected to the mother brand.
Do not imply that services are operational if they are still being developed.
Do not invent partnerships, licences, financial services, investment capabilities or client results.
5. Create an intelligent relationship journey
Replace generic lead-generation language with a discreet "Start a Relationship" experience.
Provide three primary journeys:

1. Private Clients.
2. Professional Partners.
3. Institutions and Strategic Relationships.

Each journey should use relevant, progressive qualification questions.
Capture enough information to identify high-value opportunities without asking unnecessary or sensitive questions upfront.
Design the handoff to Pipedrive and Imperial OS, including ownership, source attribution, deduplication, consent and follow-up requirements.
No simulated submission should be represented as successful delivery.
6. Strengthen institutional credibility
Use genuine Imperial events, offices, leadership and documented institutional relationships as evidence of credibility.
Publish event photography with editorial restraint and appropriate permissions.
Review all statistics and claims against supporting records.
Remove or hold anything that cannot be verified.
The site should build confidence through substance, not exaggerated numbers or visual spectacle.
7. Create a unified Imperial brand standard
Develop a reusable editorial and UX standard that can guide:

* Imperial Holdings Group.
* Imperial Citizenship.
* Imperial Partners Club.
* Imperial Private Office.
* Imperial Real Estate.
* Imperial Academy.

All divisions should share a recognisable level of sophistication, typography discipline, navigation quality, accessibility and communication standards.
Each division must retain its own audience, positioning and conversion journey.
Do not force every division into an identical website template.
8. Improve the experience without adding complexity
Preserve useful existing functionality, including site search, responsive navigation, division exploration and the Journal.
Audit interaction quality, mobile responsiveness, accessibility, loading speed and forms.
Avoid unnecessary animations, excessive overlays, complex navigation and decorative interactions that distract from the brand.
Every interaction should either improve understanding, increase confidence or help the visitor take an appropriate next step.
9. Deliverables
Provide:

1. A full copy audit with current versus proposed wording.
2. Two or three sophisticated hero messaging options.
3. A refined homepage design.
4. Updated copy across all major pages.
5. A restrained event photography plan.
6. A premium, interactive relationship enquiry journey.
7. A reusable Imperial Group brand voice and digital experience guide.
8. A launch-readiness audit identifying all unresolved placeholders, legal claims, broken links and technical issues.
9. An updated interactive website prototype for Founder approval.

Preserve the existing website and improve it intelligently.
The final objective is a world-class institutional website that inspires confidence, demonstrates the strength of Imperial's ecosystem and creates valuable new relationships, without sacrificing discretion, elegance or authenticity.
